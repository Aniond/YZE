Special Actions SRD p.16–19
Sneak Attack
Stealth roll to approach undetected first. If you succeed, the attack counts as surprising — +3 modifier in melee, and the target cannot block. Sneak attacks are individual (one attacker, one target).
Use normal Attack roll with +3 modifier.
Grapple
Declare before rolling. If attack succeeds, both you and your target fall to the ground — the opponent drops any held weapon and cannot move. To break free costs a slow action and a Melee roll. While grappling, you can only perform a grapple attack (fast action, cannot be blocked).
Block
Reactive: declare before the attacker rolls. Turns the attack into an opposed Melee roll. Each success you roll eliminates one of the attacker's successes. Costs your fast action for the round. You cannot block ranged attacks — only melee. Requires a weapon or tool to block armed or beast attacks.
Full Auto
Requires a fully automatic weapon. Counts as a normal ranged attack. If you hit, you may immediately make another attack against the same or a different target. If that hits too, you may make a third attack. After all attacks are resolved, make a supply roll for ammunition.
Use normal Attack Roll — Roll Attack button in the weapons section.
Diving Blow
Move into Engaged range AND attack in the same round. Roll Melee. If you hit, damage is increased by one. Both you and the target fall prone. You must spend a fast action to get up next round. Failure means only you fall.
Overwatch
Slow action. Aim in a specified direction — no enemies at Engaged range required. Until your next turn, you can fire when any target appears in that direction. Your shot is resolved before all other actions, even ones already declared. If two Overwatch positions target each other, an opposed Marksmanship roll determines who fires first.
Declared action — attack fires normally when triggered.
Surprise / Ambush
Fully surprising attack: you choose any initiative card instead of drawing randomly. In melee you get +3 modifier; the target cannot block. Ambush (lying in wait): each potential victim rolls Observation −2. Those who fail get the lowest initiative cards available.
Reference only — resolve via normal Attack roll with modifiers.
