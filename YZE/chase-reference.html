Maneuvers chosen secretly, then revealed
PURSUE / FLEE
Roll Mobility [Driving]. Each success moves the distance one range category toward (pursuer) or away (prey). If range exceeds Extreme after both maneuvers, the chase ends. At Engaged, pursuer may make an immediate close combat or ram attack.
HIDE — prey only
Not usable at Short range or less. Medium range: -2 modifier. Extreme range: +2 modifier. Opposed Stealth [Driving] vs pursuer's Observation. Win = chase is over, or make a sneak attack at a range of your choosing.
BLOCK — prey only
Roll Force [Driving]. Success moves one range category away, and the pursuer must also roll Force [Driving] before their own maneuver — failure cancels their maneuver.
CUT OFF — pursuer only
Opposed Mobility [Driving]. -2 if distance is Long or Extreme. Win = distance immediately Engaged + immediate attack. Lose or tie = distance increases to Extreme. If already Extreme, the chase is over.
STAND AND SHOOT
Normal ranged weapon attack. All participants on the same side can perform this maneuver. Passengers in vehicles may fire after the driver's maneuver: -2 modifier unless driver chose Stand and Shoot.
Obstacles GM rolls D10 each round
Roll after both participants secretly choose their maneuver, before revealing. Adapt obstacles to the setting as needed.
