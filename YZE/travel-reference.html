Travel Tasks SRD p.42–44
Marching
Walking on foot to progress on the travel map. Can be combined with Keeping Watch for one character only.
No roll required — automatic progress at terrain speed.
Driving
Maneuvering a vehicle on land, water, or air. Passengers can keep watch, rest, or sleep while you drive.
No roll for normal travel — Driving roll only for difficult conditions.
Foraging
Looking for edible plants and herbs. Must be done on foot. Roll Survival modified by terrain foraging modifier. Each success = 1 ration found.
Hunting
Looking for game to hunt. Must be done on foot. Roll Survival or Marksmanship modified by terrain hunting modifier. Each success = D6 rations.
Keeping Watch
Scouting for enemies and other encounters. Only one character can perform this task per quarter-day. Can be combined with marching.
Gathering
Collecting wood or grain to produce alcohol fuel. Must be done on foot.
No roll required — automatic if terrain allows.
Fishing
Catching fish in lakes and rivers. Roll Survival. Each success = D6 rations. Lakes and rivers only.
Making Camp
Setting up a camp for the night. The camp provides shelter and allows the party to sleep and perform other rest activities.
No roll required — automatic. Sleeping in the open without a camp is possible but risky in harsh conditions.
Sleeping
Resting for a shift. Required to heal Wits stress and avoid the Sleep Deprivation condition. Must sleep at least one shift per day.
No roll required.

River & Lake Travel SRD p.45
Rivers and lakes can be used for traveling. Fords and bridges allow crossing a river on foot. Without them, you need a raft or boat — or must swim across.
Swimming: see Drowning in the Hazards reference. Metal armor = sink automatically. Stamina roll every stretch to stay afloat; every round under water.
Speed x1 on water with a vessel. Canoe/raft: +2 Driving modifier.
Roads
Traveling along a major road is generally faster but increases encounter risk. Only main roads count as roads for game purposes.
Speed factor is always x1 on roads regardless of the underlying terrain type. The road’s Driving modifier (+3) applies to vehicle travel on the road.
Foraging and hunting on a road use the modifiers of the surrounding terrain — roads don’t improve or worsen those rolls.
