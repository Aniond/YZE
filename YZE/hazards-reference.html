Environmental Hazards
GM reference — SRD p.24–25. Each roll uses Stamina unless noted. Dice pool: set dice count to match the hazard's intensity/virulence.
Fire
Roll for the fire's intensity. Each success = 1 damage. If damaged, you catch fire and burn again on your turn each round (intensity rises by 1 each round). Fire goes out when an attack inflicts no damage. Put out with Mobility (slow action; +2 with blanket).
Broken by fire: death save every round until saved by Healing. Crits not rolled — unique death-save sequence applies.
Intensity guide (dice pool / step dice): 3(D)=torch/clothes · 6(C)=campfire/burning building · 9(B)=bonfire · 12(A)=furnace
Falling
Damage = meters fallen ÷ 2, rounding fractions down. Armor mitigates. Controlled jump: roll Mobility — each success reduces damage by 1.
Example: falling 6 m = 3 damage. No Stamina roll — damage is automatic.
Drowning
Stamina roll every stretch to stay afloat. Metal armor = sink automatically. Under water: Stamina every round to hold breath. Fail = 1 Strength damage per round until saved. Broken while drowning = die after one stretch.
Poison
Opposed Stamina vs toxicity. Fail = effect below. Antidotes halt effects.
Toxicity: weak=3(D) · strong=6(C) · extremely potent=9(B)+
Lethal: 1 damage/round until broken, then roll again or die.
Paralyzing: No move or actions for one stretch.
Sleeping: Fall asleep for one shift.
Disease
Opposed Stamina vs virulence (infection roll). Typical virulence: 6(C). Fail = ill: 1 damage, no natural healing, roll again each day — each fail = 1 more damage. Broken while sick = die next day without aid. Succeed = no longer ill. Healer rolls instead of patient with +2 modifier.
Cold
Stamina roll at intervals (frequency increases with severity: chilly day=per day, winter=per shift, extreme=per stretch). Extra protection like a blanket gives +2.
Fail = 1 damage + 1 stress, no natural healing. Keep rolling. Broken while hypothermic = die the next time you must roll. Recovery only after warming up.
Starvation
Must eat 1 ration per day. Starving: no Strength healing. After 1 week without food: 1 damage per week. Broken while starving = die after another week. Eating ends starvation immediately. Stress (other attributes) heals normally.
Sleep Deprivation
Must sleep 1 shift per day. Without sleep: cannot heal Wits damage. Broken by Wits stress while deprived = collapse, sleep 1 shift, cannot be woken. Recover after sleeping at least 1 shift.
Explosions
Blast power = dice to roll. Short range: roll blast dice — each success = 1 damage (base damage + extras). Engaged range: target suffers +1 extra damage minimum. Medium range: blast power reduced by 6 (if nothing blocks). Armor protects.
Blast power / base damage: 3(D)=1 · 6(C)=2 · 9(B)=3 · 12(A)=4
Mounts SRD p.25
Movement
Mobility roll each move action: each success = +1 additional zone moved. Use mount's Agility, not rider's. Cannot enter Cramped zones. Cluttered zones: max 1 zone per action.
Close Combat
One-handed weapons only from horseback. Opponents choose to attack rider or mount. Attacks against a mounted rider suffer -1 penalty.
Ranged Combat
All ranged attacks from horseback suffer -2. Opponents choose to target rider or mount.
Damage
Mount takes damage normally; recovers like a PC. Strength reduced to 0 = perished (no crit roll). Mounts generally lack Wits or Empathy.
